Map of countries: Gender in School Textbooks

Map of countries

National textbooks, 2,633 books in the model. Each country is shaded by its female share of gendered words after adjusting for subject, grade and book length (the paper’s model): amber below parity, where men are more of the words, teal above it. Hatched countries have fewer than 10 books in the model, so are not ranked; blank countries have no books.

Not shown at this scale: Bahrain; Cabo Verde; Dominica; Grenada; Kiribati; Maldives; Malta; Mauritius; Micronesia, Fed. Sts.; Samoa; Seychelles; St. Kitts and Nevis; Tonga.
