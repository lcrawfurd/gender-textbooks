Each country’s female share of gendered words in its national textbooks, adjusted for subject, grade and book length.

National textbooks, 2,806 books in the model. Each dot is a country’s female share of gendered words after adjusting for subject, grade and book length (the paper’s model); lines are 95% intervals; open dots are countries added since the paper. Countries with fewer than 5 books in the model are pooled into one level of the model, as the paper’s “Other” was, and countries with fewer than 10 are listed below the chart, not ranked.
