Each panel puts countries on the same vertical scale of female share; the band above 50% (teal) means more women, below it (amber) more men, and the pale line is the fitted line. The line’s slope and the 95% interval in each caption come from the books: a regression of each book’s female share on its country’s characteristic, with each country weighted equally, a term for each subject and grade band, and standard errors clustered by country; the line is drawn through the countries’ average. The correlation is the countries’.
