Over time: Gender in School Textbooks

Over time

National textbooks published from 2000 on (1,113 books; 9 with an earlier year and 12 with no gendered words left out), compared within countries and adjusted for book length. 834 national textbooks have no publication year and are not in this chart.

The female share changes by +0.6 points a decade within countries (95% interval −3.4 to +4.7), so the data cannot rule out no change.
