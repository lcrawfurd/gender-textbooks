Over time: Gender in School Textbooks

Over time

National textbooks published from 2000 on (1,677 books; 9 with an earlier year and 99 with no gendered words left out), compared within countries and adjusted for book length. The chart also counts 170 earlier editions of books the site holds in a newer one (see the methods). 1,440 national textbooks have no publication year and are not in this chart. The female share changes by −0.1 points a decade within countries (95% interval −2.6 to +2.5), so the data cannot rule out no change.

Dots are the adjusted shares; lines are 95% intervals.
