Kenya

Gender in Kenya’s school textbooks: what the site finds in its national textbooks, in their words and in their pictures. Figures as of 26 November 2026.

Words

How often women and girls are mentioned in Kenya’s 12 English national textbooks. Adjusted for subject, grade and book length (the paper’s model), Kenya’s share is the fourth highest of the 41 countries ranked.

    national textbooks scored.

By subject

Only one subject group, Maths, has at least 200 gendered words in Kenya’s national textbooks: 40.7% of them are female, over 3 books.

By grade

Only one grade band, primary (grades 1–6), has at least 200 gendered words in Kenya’s national textbooks: 53.4% of them are female, over 12 books.

How the figures are made

The words figures count gendered nouns and pronouns (she, mother, queen; he, father, king) in each national textbook, the method of the 2024 paper below, applied to the books found since; the adjusted share comes from a regression of each book’s female share on country, subject group, grade and book length. The pictures come from a model (Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite) that coded 20 random pages of each national textbook, in any language, following a written coding manual (pages a first look found no person on were not coded further); second copies of a book are counted once, and every disability flag was checked by eye.
