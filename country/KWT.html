Kuwait

Gender in Kuwait’s school textbooks: what the living paper finds in its national textbooks, in their words and in their pictures. Figures as of 12 November 2026.

Words

How often women and girls are mentioned in Kuwait’s 34 English national textbooks. Adjusted for subject, grade and book length (the paper’s model), Kuwait’s share is the seventh highest of the 41 countries ranked.

  • 49.1%

    of gendered words refer to women and girls, adjusted for subject, grade and book length (95% interval 43.0–55.3).

  • 39.7%

    of gendered words in all its national textbooks refer to women and girls (pooled, no adjustment).

  • 34

    national textbooks scored, added since the 2024 paper.

Pictures

A model looked at 20 random pages of each of Kuwait’s 300 national textbooks, in every language, and recorded each person pictured.

How the figures are made

The words figures count gendered nouns and pronouns (she, mother, queen; he, father, king) in each national textbook, the method of the 2024 paper below, applied to the books found since; the adjusted share comes from a regression of each book’s female share on country, subject group, grade and book length. The pictures come from a model (Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite) that coded 20 random pages of each national textbook, in any language, following a written coding manual (pages a first look found no person on were not coded further); second copies of a book are counted once; the model’s disability flags are not yet checked by eye, so no disability figures are shown.
