Gender in Malawi’s school textbooks: what the site finds in its national textbooks, in their words and in their pictures. Figures as of 26 November 2026.

Words

How often women and girls are mentioned in Malawi’s 5 English national textbooks. With 4 books in the model, fewer than 10, Malawi is not ranked: its adjusted share would be too uncertain.

Only one subject group, Language, has at least 200 gendered words in Malawi’s national textbooks: 54.9% of them are female, over 4 books.

By grade

Only one grade band, primary (grades 1–6), has at least 200 gendered words in Malawi’s national textbooks: 53.6% of them are female, over 5 books.

How the figures are made

The words figures count gendered nouns and pronouns (she, mother, queen; he, father, king) in each national textbook, the method of the 2024 paper below, applied to the books found since; the adjusted share comes from a regression of each book’s female share on country, subject group, grade and book length. The pictures come from a model (Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite) that coded 20 random pages of each national textbook, in any language, following a written coding manual (pages a first look found no person on were not coded further); second copies of a book are counted once, and every disability flag was checked by eye.
