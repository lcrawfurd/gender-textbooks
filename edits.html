Small edits, fewer stereotypes

Most of the stereotypes in textbooks sit in a word or a drawing that could easily be otherwise. Here are real examples from national textbooks, each beside a version with one small change.

Sentences

Sentences from national textbooks, beside a version with a word or two changed.

A job given only to men

In the textbook

When a scientist in one country catches a migrating bird, he puts a metal ring on its leg.

Revised

When scientists in one country catch a migrating bird, they put a metal ring on its leg.

Sudan · grade 10 · English

A job given only to men

In the textbook

For example, the doctor wears a stethoscope around his neck.

Revised

For example, the doctor wears a stethoscope around her neck.

Rwanda · grade 8 · English

A job given only to men

In the textbook

A businessman borrowed BHD15,000 from his bank for 6 years at simple interest rate 5% annually.

Revised

A businesswoman borrowed BHD15,000 from her bank for 6 years at simple interest rate 5% annually.

Bahrain · grade Secondary · Financial mathematics

Women at home, men outside

In the textbook

Father is in the backyard and mother is in the kitchen.

Revised

Father is in the kitchen and mother is in the backyard.

Cuba · grade 7 · English

Women at home, men outside

In the textbook

My mother (already finish) her housework by then, and she (probably listen) to the radio, but my father (still work) in his office.

Revised

My father (already finish) his housework by then, and he (probably listen) to the radio, but my mother (still work) in her office.

Tajikistan · grade 11 · English
