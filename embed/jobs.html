The jobs they are shown in: Gender in School Textbooks

The jobs they are shown in

Women are 28.9% of the people the books show in a job (95% interval 25.9–34.7). Among the jobs that can be matched to labour-market data, women are 28.8% of the people shown, against 37.5% of the same jobs in the labour market (ILOSTAT): a gap of −8.7 points (95% interval −12.2 to −6.5). Women are also shown in more female-typed jobs than men: in the labour market, the jobs women are shown in are 5.0 points more female than the jobs men are shown in (95% interval +3.2 to +5.9).

Dots are women’s share of the people the books show in a job that can be matched to labour-market data; squares are women’s share of the same jobs in the labour market (ILOSTAT); the line joins the two. All national textbooks, then by World Bank region and income group.
