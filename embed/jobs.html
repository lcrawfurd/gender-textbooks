The jobs they are shown in: Gender in School Textbooks

The jobs they are shown in

Women are 28.9% of the people the books show in a job (95% interval 25.9–34.8). Among the jobs that can be matched to labour-market data, women are 28.8% of the people shown, against 37.5% of the same jobs in the labour market (ILOSTAT): a gap of −8.8 points (95% interval −12.3 to −6.6). Women are also shown in more female-typed jobs than men: in the labour market, the jobs women are shown in are 5.0 points more female than the jobs men are shown in (95% interval +3.2 to +5.9).
