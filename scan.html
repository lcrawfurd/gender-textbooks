Scan your book

Soon you will be able to upload a textbook (a PDF, or a folder of them) and get the same counts as every book here, set against the books of its country and subject.

What it will report

  • The share of gendered words that refer to women and girls.
  • Words about children and about adults, separately.
  • Who is the subject of a sentence and who is its object.
  • The jobs women and men are shown doing.

How your files will be handled

Each file will be deleted as soon as its scan finishes. Only the counts are kept, and a book is added to this site only if you ask and it is publicly available.

Until then

Ministries, publishers and researchers who would like books scanned now can contact Lee Crawfurd at the Center for Global Development.
