Words near women and men: Gender in School Textbooks

Words near women and men

Around each mention of a woman or a man (the same sentence, up to ten words either side), the 2024 paper’s theme words, counted per 100 mentions of women and of men. Appearance and family words sit nearer women; achievement words sit nearer men. For work words the data cannot rule out no gap. Two checks that use word vectors instead of counting words point the same way for all 4 themes: see Methods.

Each row gives a theme’s words per 100 mentions of women (dots) and of men (squares), over all national textbooks; the line joins the two. Themes are ordered from nearer women to nearer men.
